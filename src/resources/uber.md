## Study Tips

- **Focus on high-frequency problems first** - These are most likely to appear in interviews
- **Practice graph algorithms** - Many Uber problems involve routing and navigation
- **Master dynamic programming** - Several problems require DP solutions
- **Understand data structures** - BST, Trie, and Hash Map are commonly used
- **Practice system design** - Uber often asks about scalable system design
